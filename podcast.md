# Basic Block Radio

I co-host [Basic Block Radio](https://basicblockradio.com/) («Базовый Блок») with Ivan Ivanitskiy and Alexander Seleznev.
It is a technical blockchain podcast in Russian.

I started it in 2017, during my PhD at the University of Luxembourg, to explain the research I was doing.
It grew into long-form interviews with protocol researchers, core developers, and founders.
We also recap industry reports and conferences.

Guests have included:

* Anatoly Yakovenko (Solana)
* Sergey Nazarov (Chainlink)
* Alex Skidanov (NEAR)
* Dmitry Khovratovich (Ethereum Foundation)
* Alex Gluchowski (Matter Labs)
* Vasiliy Shapovalov (Lido)
* Mikhail Kudinov (Blockstream)

Listen on [basicblockradio.com](https://basicblockradio.com/).
